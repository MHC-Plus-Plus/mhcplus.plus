Ayesha is a Computer Science student and Moynihan Public Service Fellow at CCNY. She’s passionate about using technology to create meaningful impact and is especially interested in exploring how computer science can intersect with public policy and administration to serve the public interest. She previously interned at BlackRock, a multinational asset management firm.

Ayesha has been involved with MHC++ where she has served as Treasurer during her first two years and now helps organize events and create opportunities for students to connect with one another. She currently works with the CUNY Central Office of Academic Affairs, supporting the implementation of the Universal Transfer Path for computer science across CUNY.

In her free time, she enjoys nonfiction books, black tea, and a challenging hike.
