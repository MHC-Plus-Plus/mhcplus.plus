---
title: About MHC++
---

MHC++ is the inter-CUNY computer science club, based at Macaulay Honors College and open to students at every CUNY campus.

CUNY is one of the largest and most diverse public university systems in the country, but its CS students rarely meet across campuses. We exist to change that, through career development, mutual growth, and lasting connection.

We run workshops, talks, and meetups, and we organize HackMHC++, our own hackathon. Whatever campus you're at and whatever year you're in, there's a place for you here.
