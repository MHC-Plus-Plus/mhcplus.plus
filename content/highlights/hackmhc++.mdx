HackMHC++ 2026 was MHC++'s inaugural hackathon, and it was a huge success! 75+ hackers came to the Macaulay Honors building for a weekend of project-building, workshopping, and eating great food 😋.

We are very grateful to the sponsors who helped make this event possible, and hope to make the next HackMHC++ even better!
